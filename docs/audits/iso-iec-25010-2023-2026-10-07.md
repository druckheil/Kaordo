# Kaordo quality audit and refactor — ISO/IEC 25010:2023

**Assessment date:** 7 October 2026 UTC. **Baseline:** `c061186` on `scope-0.0.3`; results apply to the accompanying refactor. **Quality Score: 95.9/100.**

The requested ≥99 overall, every characteristic/subcharacteristic ≥95 and no Critical/High findings is **not met**. No unresolved Critical/High code defect was confirmed in the reviewed scope after the fixes. **One High operational finding remains: production has no independent versioned recovery copy (F1).** A successful disposable backup test and two mirrored disks do not close that finding. The scores below describe evidence, not an ISO certificate or a promise of bug-free software.

## Scope, method and acceptance

The assessment covers the implemented Portal account/identity entry, Fluo posting/notifications/privacy, Ligo conversations, Rondo communities/calls/device settings, Lingvo private German dictionaries/practice and Regado administration; shared browser packages; Kerno, Nodo, mediaauth and regado-agent; contracts, migrations, dependencies, tests and local/production configuration. Reserved Matrix/Cloudflare integration, broader notifications/settings, content encryption and escrow are disclosed rather than treated as implemented features or arbitrary missing requirements. Existing private content assumes a trusted server operator.

The [ISO/IEC 25010:2023 product quality model](https://www.iso.org/standard/78176.html) defines nine characteristics. The 40 names follow that model; numeric scoring and equal weighting are this assessment's engineering method. A subcharacteristic is evaluated against applicable controls, reproducible normal/failure checks and remaining evidence gaps. This is a review of the existing implementation, including its configured operational design; it does not manufacture new product requirements to lower or raise the score.

Rubric: 0–49 absent/unverified; 50–69 partial controls; 70–84 working capability with a material unclosed risk; 85–94 integrated capability with material operating/measurement gaps; 95–98 strong representative automated evidence and explicit limited residual gaps; 99–100 sufficiently complete evidence for the declared conditions with no material unresolved gap. Test pass rates and statement coverage are reported separately, never substituted for quality scores. Unknown production scale, user experience or recovery is not assigned 100 merely because local tests pass.

For characteristic `c`, `score(c) = sum(subcharacteristic scores) / number of subcharacteristics`. The overall score is the unweighted mean of the **unrounded nine characteristic means**, rounded once to one decimal. No failed category is hidden by weighting. Historical October 1 and UI audit scores have different scopes and are not current certification.
